About This Project

A personal research tool tracking the business environment for the U.S. defense sector. Built to surface data that is publicly available but rarely aggregated in one place.

Sources

Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED), Yahoo Finance, U.S. Census Bureau, and curated news intelligence from defense-sector publications.

Coverage & Updates

Indicators cover 2019–present (monthly unless otherwise noted). Deal tracker and indicator data refresh automatically on a daily basis.

Scope

U.S. defense sector. Excludes classified programs, foreign defense markets, and government-to-government transfers.

Methodology

Investment data is custom-compiled from public filings and reports. Macro indicators are sourced directly from official statistical releases with no adjustments.
