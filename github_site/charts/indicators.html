Defense Capital Goods Orders

Manufacturers' new orders for defense capital goods

Manufacturers' New Orders: Defense Capital Goods (ADEFNO) is a data set released monthly by the US Census Bureau. This data set tracks how much money US manufacturers are receiving in new orders for military equipment and technology, from aircraft and missiles to small arms and communication systems. It gives us a snapshot of how active the defense industry is at any given time.

Source: Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED)

Durable Goods Orders

Manufacturers' new orders for durable goods across the U.S. economy

Manufacturers' New Orders: Durable Goods (DGORDER) is a broad monthly indicator from the US Census Bureau tracking new orders received by manufacturers for goods expected to last three years or more. It covers the full U.S. industrial economy — machinery, electronics, transportation, and defense — and serves as a leading indicator of manufacturing activity and business investment.

Source: Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED)
